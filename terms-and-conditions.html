Section 01 // Eligibility & Enrollment

A. Tournament Registration

Registration for the VYRA Free Fire Championship is governed by strict capacity brackets and qualification requirements. By submitting a registration, participants agree to the following terms:

  • Slot Availability: Tournament enrollment is strictly subject to available lobby bracket capacity. Once bracket capacity reaches its cap, registration for that tier is closed immediately.
  • Accurate Information: All participants must submit truthful, complete, and accurate information during registration, including participant name, valid 10-digit Indian mobile number, in-game Free Fire nickname (IGN), and Free Fire character UID.
  • Provisional vs. Confirmed Status: Completing a registration form or initiating checkout does not constitute a guaranteed slot. A tournament booking is confirmed only after the website system validates the transaction and issues an official Digital Ticket with a verified Ticket ID.
  • Right to Refuse & Disqualify: VYRA Esports reserves the express right to reject, void, or disqualify any registration that contains duplicate numbers, misleading details, bot-generated data, fake player UIDs, or fraudulent submissions.

Section 03 // Critical Payment Policy

C. Non-Refundable Payments

⚠️ IMPORTANT: ALL CONFIRMED TOURNAMENT BOOKINGS ARE NON-REFUNDABLE.
Once a tournament booking has been confirmed and a Digital Ticket issued by VYRA Esports, the registration fee is strictly non-refundable and cannot be refunded for a participant's change of mind, personal scheduling conflict, absence, late arrival, disqualification due to a rule violation, or failure to attend.

Participants must review this policy with care prior to completing checkout:

  • Finality of Registration: Because tournament slot reservations directly allocate custom match room lobby positions, referee bandwidth, and prize pool calculations, confirmed registration payments cannot be returned or cancelled once verified.
  • Pre-Payment Review Obligation: Participants are required to thoroughly review their chosen pass category, squad roster, mobile contact, and payment details before confirming their booking.
  • Exclusions from Refund Eligibility: Non-attendance, team disputes, internet outages, phone battery failure, device incompatibility, or match lobby disqualification do not constitute grounds for a refund.
  • Statutory Rights & Event Cancellation: This policy does not exclude, limit, or modify any statutory consumer guarantees, remedy, or legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law. If VYRA Esports cancels the tournament or cannot provide the promised event, refunds or appropriate remedies will be administered in accordance with applicable law and the specific circumstances. We do not claim that payments are non-refundable regardless of the reason.
Section 04 // Competition Integrity

D. Tournament Rules & Fair Play

Fair play is the core foundation of VYRA Esports. All registered players are bound by tournament conduct rules:

  • Adherence to Official Directions: Competitors must strictly comply with all tournament rules, referee announcements, and scheduled lobby check-in windows.
  • Anti-Cheat Zero Tolerance: Any form of cheating, third-party aimbots, script injectors, modified APK files, glitch exploits, emulator bypasses, smurfing, account sharing, or illegal teaming with opposing squads will result in immediate match disqualification and permanent platform ban without refund.
  • Harassment & Toxicity: Harassment, abusive language, hate speech, or unsportsmanlike conduct directed toward referees, staff, or opposing competitors is strictly prohibited.
  • Adjudication & Dispute Resolution: Match referees hold final authority over kill points, placement scores, and rule interpretations based strictly on published tournament standards.

Section 05 // Credential Security & Access

E. Digital Tickets & Entry

Upon successful payment verification, the system issues a tamper-evident Digital Pass:

  • Credential Security: Each participant is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their unique Ticket ID (e.g., VYRA-TKT-XXXXXX) and dynamic digital QR code. Do not share credentials publicly.
  • Authorized Allocation: Each issued Squad Pass permits entry for exactly one 4-player squad roster.
  • Duplicate & Counterfeit Rejection: Duplicate, photocopied, screenshot-shared, forwarded, or previously redeemed passes will be automatically flagged and rejected by tournament verification gates.
  • Official Staff Verification: Admission to match lobbies and distribution of custom room ID and password credentials is strictly contingent on identity and phone verification by authorized tournament officials.
